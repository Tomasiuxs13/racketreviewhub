Adidas Adipower Lite

2025 Edition

The Adidas Adipower Lite 2025 is designed for intermediate players seeking high responsiveness and technical progress. Featuring a teardrop shape with medium balance and a flexible carbon face, this racket balances power and control effectively. The Adipower Lite proves that you can have excellent responsiveness without sacrificing too much power or control.

Technical Analysis

Shape and Balance

The Adipower Lite features a teardrop shape with medium balance, creating a balanced racket that adapts to different playing situations. The teardrop shape provides a good balance between power and control, while the medium balance ensures the racket feels balanced without favoring one aspect. This combination makes the racket ideal for intermediate players developing their game.

Materials and Core

The Adipower Lite utilizes a carbon fiber frame with flexible carbon faces and an EVA Soft core. The flexible carbon surface provides enhanced responsiveness and feel, while the EVA Soft core offers comfort and good energy return. The lighter weight (360g) makes it easy to handle for extended play sessions.

Technologies

Adidas's flexible carbon technology enhances responsiveness and feel, while the EVA Soft core provides excellent comfort. The combination of these technologies creates a racket that delivers high responsiveness with good balance between power and control.

On the Court

The Adipower Lite offers excellent responsiveness from the back, thanks to its flexible face and balanced design. Intermediate players will appreciate the balance between power and control, allowing them to develop their game effectively. The flexible face enhances feel, making it easier to learn proper technique and improve your game.

At the net, the Adipower Lite provides good performance with excellent responsiveness. The high responsiveness makes it easier to react to fast shots, and the balanced design ensures consistent performance. Intermediate players will find the racket easy to use and forgiving at the net.

On Smash

The smash power is good for intermediate players, thanks to the teardrop shape and quality construction.
