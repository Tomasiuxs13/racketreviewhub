Nox ML10 Pro Cup

2025 Edition

The Nox ML10 Pro Cup 2025 is one of the most legendary rackets in padel, renowned for its exceptional balance and control. Used by professional player Miguel Lamperti, this racket has achieved cult status among padel players for its versatility and consistent performance. Featuring a teardrop shape with medium balance, the ML10 Pro Cup proves that perfect balance can create a racket that excels in all aspects of the game.

Technical Analysis

Shape and Balance

The ML10 Pro Cup features a teardrop shape with medium balance, creating a versatile racket that adapts to different playing situations. The teardrop shape provides a perfect balance between power and control, while the medium balance ensures the racket feels balanced without favoring one aspect. This combination makes the racket ideal for players who value versatility.

Materials and Core

The ML10 Pro Cup utilizes a carbon fiber frame with carbon fiber faces and an EVA Soft core. The carbon fiber construction ensures durability and responsiveness, while the EVA Soft core provides a comfortable feel that enhances control. This combination creates a racket that's both powerful and comfortable.

Technologies

Nox's technologies focus on creating perfect balance. The teardrop shape and medium balance work together to create a racket that excels in all aspects of play. The EVA Soft core provides excellent comfort, while the carbon fiber construction ensures consistent performance.
