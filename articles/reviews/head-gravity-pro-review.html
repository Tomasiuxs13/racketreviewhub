Head Gravity Pro

2025 Edition

The Head Gravity Pro 2025 represents Head's commitment to control-focused design, featuring a unique flip design that enhances stability and precision. With a round shape and low balance, this racket excels in defensive play and precise shot placement. The Gravity Pro proves that maximum control can be achieved without sacrificing too much power when the design is carefully balanced.

Technical Analysis

Shape and Balance

The Gravity Pro features a round shape with low balance, creating a control-focused racket that excels in precision. The round shape provides a large sweet spot and excellent control, while the low balance enhances maneuverability and allows for quicker reactions. This combination makes the racket ideal for defensive players and those who prioritize placement.

Materials and Core

The Gravity Pro utilizes a carbon fiber frame with carbon fiber faces and an EVA Soft core. The carbon fiber construction ensures durability and responsiveness, while the EVA Soft core provides a comfortable feel that enhances control. The unique flip design enhances stability and reduces unwanted vibrations.

Technologies

Head's Graphene 360+ technology enhances stability and energy transfer, while the unique flip design provides additional stability. The Smart Bridge adapts to different playing styles, ensuring consistent performance. The combination of these technologies creates a racket that offers exceptional control with excellent stability.

On the Court

From the Back of the Court

The Gravity Pro truly excels from the back of the court. The large sweet spot and low balance make it exceptionally easy to control defensive shots and maintain rallies. The unique flip design enhances stability, making it easier to place the ball precisely where you want it. This makes the racket ideal for players who rely on strategy and placement over power.

The round shape provides excellent forgiveness on off-center hits, and the low balance allows for quick reactions to fast shots. This makes the racket perfect for defensive players who need to control the pace of the game from the back.

At the Net

At the net, the Gravity Pro offers excellent precision for volleys and blocks. While it doesn't have the explosive power of power rackets, it provides enough power for effective net play when combined with good technique. The low balance and round shape make it easy to react quickly to fast exchanges.

The racket excels at precise placement at the net, allowing you to place volleys exactly where you want them. The excellent maneuverability makes it ideal for quick reactions and defensive blocks. The unique flip design enhances stability during fast exchanges.

On Smash

The smash power is good but not exceptional, as expected from a control-focused racket. The round shape and low balance reduce power compared to diamond-shaped rackets, but the quality construction ensures that you can still hit effective smashes with proper technique. The racket rewards good timing and positioning with solid power.

While you won't get the devastating power of power rackets, the Gravity Pro provides enough power for effective smashes when you're in position. The focus here is on placement and consistency rather than raw power.

Performance Breakdown

Power (7.5/10)

The Gravity Pro offers good power, though significantly less than power-focused rackets. The round shape and low balance prioritize control over power, but the quality construction ensures that power remains adequate for competitive play.

Control (9.2/10)

Control is exceptional, which is the Gravity Pro's primary strength. The round shape, large sweet spot, and unique flip design combine to create one of the most controllable rackets available. Placement is precise, and the racket responds excellently to your intentions.

Rebound (8.0/10)

The EVA Soft core and carbon fiber construction provide good energy return. While not as explosive as power rackets, the rebound quality supports both defensive and offensive play effectively. The comfortable feel enhances the overall playing experience.

Maneuverability (8.8/10)

Maneuverability is excellent, one of the Gravity Pro's strongest features. The low balance makes the racket feel light and responsive, allowing for quick reactions and fast swings. The unique flip design enhances stability without sacrificing maneuverability.

Sweet Spot (9.0/10)

The round shape creates a large sweet spot that's very forgiving. The unique flip design enhances stability, making it easier to find the sweet spot consistently. This makes the racket easier to use and more consistent, especially for intermediate players.

Who It's For

The Head Gravity Pro is designed for advanced players who:

  • Prioritize control and precision over power
  • Play defensively and rely on placement
  • Want excellent maneuverability
  • Need a forgiving racket with a large sweet spot
  • Value consistency and precision in their game

This racket is not recommended for players who prioritize maximum power and aggressive play, as the control-focused design sacrifices power for precision.

Conclusion

The Head Gravity Pro 2025 is an excellent choice for players who prioritize control and precision. Its round shape, low balance, and unique flip design create a racket that excels in defensive play and precise placement, while maintaining the premium quality Head is known for.

For advanced players who value control over power, the Gravity Pro offers exceptional performance. The excellent maneuverability and large sweet spot make it forgiving and easy to use, while the premium construction ensures consistent performance. If you're looking for maximum control with unique design features, the Gravity Pro is an outstanding choice.
