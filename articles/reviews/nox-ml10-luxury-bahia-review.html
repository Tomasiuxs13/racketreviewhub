The ML10 Luxury Bahia features a teardrop shape with medium balance, creating a versatile racket that adapts well to different playing situations. This combination makes the racket ideal for players who value versatility and adaptability.

Materials and Core

The ML10 Luxury Bahia utilizes a carbon fiber frame with carbon fiber faces and an EVA Soft core. The carbon fiber construction ensures durability and responsiveness, while the EVA Soft core provides a comfortable feel that enhances control.
