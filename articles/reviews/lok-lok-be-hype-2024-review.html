On the Court

From the Back of the Court

Playing from the back with this racket requires refined technique due to its smaller sweet spot and high balance. The power-oriented design means you need excellent timing to defend effectively, but rewards clean contact with exceptional power and the ability to transition from defense to offense quickly.

The diamond shape and high balance make it demanding from the back, requiring consistent contact. However, when you connect cleanly, the power is exceptional, allowing you to hit deep, penetrating shots that put pressure on your opponents.

At the Net

At the net, this racket truly shines. The aggressive design grants exceptional power on volleys and trays. Despite its emphasis on power, it provides adequate control for precise net play, making each block or consecutive volley an assertive statement.

The high balance and diamond shape excel at the net, where aggressive players can dominate. Quick exchanges favor this racket, and the power allows you to finish points decisively.

On Smash

This is where the racket truly excels. With exceptional energy transfer, it enables devastating smashes and overheads, helping to finish off points with flair and ease. The combination of the diamond shape, high balance, and carbon fiber construction creates explosive power on smashes.

Advanced players will find the power unmatched, allowing them to end points quickly and decisively. The racket rewards aggressive play with devastating results.
