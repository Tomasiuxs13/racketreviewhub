2025 Edition

The Adidas Cross It 2024 2025 represents Adidas's most uncompromising power racket, designed specifically for physically strong players who can handle its demanding nature. Technical Analysis

Shape and Balance

The Cross It 2024 features a diamond shape with high balance, creating a power-focused racket that excels in aggressive play. The diamond shape concentrates weight in the upper portion for maximum power generation, while the high balance favors powerful smashes and aggressive volleys. The heavier weight (375g) makes it ideal for physically strong players.

Materials and Core

The Cross It 2024 utilizes a 15K carbon fiber frame with 15K carbon fiber faces and a hard Adidas rubber core. The 15K carbon fiber provides exceptional stiffness and energy transfer, while the hard rubber core creates a very hard feel that maximizes power. This combination creates a racket that's uncompromising in its power delivery.

Technologies

Adidas's 15K carbon fiber technology provides maximum stiffness and energy transfer, while the hard rubber core ensures that every bit of energy is transferred to the ball. The combination of these technologies creates a racket that delivers raw, uncompromising power for physically strong players.

On the Court

From the Back of the Court

The Cross It 2024 offers uncompromising power from the back, though it requires excellent technique and physical strength to control. The diamond shape, high balance, and hard core demand precision, but when you connect cleanly, the results are devastating. The racket generates exceptional power on defensive shots, allowing you to turn defense into offense immediately.

Physically strong players will appreciate the raw power available from the back, though the demanding nature requires excellent technique. The racket rewards strength and good timing with exceptional power, making it ideal for aggressive players with the physical capability to handle it.

At the Net

At the net, the Cross It 2024 provides uncompromising power on volleys and trays. The high balance and diamond shape deliver devastating volleys, allowing you to finish points decisively. Despite its power focus, the racket maintains enough control for precise net play when you're in position and have the strength to control it.

Quick exchanges at the net favor this racket for strong players, and the power allows you to dominate net play completely. The hard feel ensures that every volley carries maximum weight, making it extremely difficult for opponents to handle your shots.

On Smash

This is where the Cross It 2024 truly excels. The combination of diamond shape, high balance, hard rubber core, and 15K carbon fiber creates the most explosive power available. The racket transfers energy with maximum efficiency, enabling devastating overheads that can end points immediately.

Physically strong players will find the smash power unmatched, allowing them to finish points with absolute authority. The Cross It 2024 rewards strength and aggressive play with exceptional results, making it ideal for players who want uncompromising power.

Performance Breakdown

Power (9.2/10)

The Cross It 2024 excels in power generation, representing one of the most powerful rackets available. The diamond shape, high balance, hard rubber core, and 15K carbon fiber combine to create uncompromising power. This is perfect for physically strong players who want maximum power.

Control (8.0/10)

While power is the Cross It 2024's absolute priority, control is still good for a racket of this power level. The hard rubber core provides some feedback, and the carbon frame offers responsiveness. Placement is precise when you're in position and have the strength to control it, though the demanding nature requires excellent technique.

Rebound (8.5/10)

The hard rubber core and 15K carbon fiber construction provide exceptional energy return. The racket responds with maximum efficiency to ball impact, transferring energy for the most powerful shots possible. The rebound quality supports the aggressive playing style perfectly.

Maneuverability (7.8/10)

Maneuverability is acceptable for a power racket of this caliber. The heavier weight and high balance make it less maneuverable than lighter rackets, but physically strong players will find it manageable. The 15K carbon fiber keeps the frame responsive despite the weight.

Sweet Spot (7.5/10)

The diamond shape and hard core result in a smaller sweet spot that requires precision and strength. This makes the racket very demanding, but it rewards clean contact with the most powerful shots available. The hard feel requires excellent technique to use effectively.

Who It's For

The Adidas Cross It 2024 is designed for physically strong advanced players who:
