Materials and Core

The AT10 Luxury Genius Arena utilizes a carbon fiber frame with rough carbon fiber faces and an EVA Soft core. The rough carbon fiber surface is the key differentiator, providing enhanced grip on the ball for increased spin generation. The carbon fiber construction ensures durability and responsiveness, while the EVA Soft core provides a comfortable feel that enhances control.

Technologies

At the net, the AT10 Luxury Genius Arena provides excellent performance with enhanced spin capabilities.
