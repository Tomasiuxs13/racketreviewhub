Adidas Metalbone 3.4

2025 Edition

The Adidas Metalbone 3.4 2025 represents the evolution of Adidas's power-focused Metalbone series, designed for aggressive players who demand maximum power. Used by professional player Alejandro Galán, this racket combines a diamond shape with high balance to create one of the most powerful rackets in Adidas's lineup. The Metalbone 3.4 proves that Adidas can deliver exceptional power while maintaining the quality and feel the brand is known for.

Technical Analysis

Shape and Balance

The Metalbone 3.4 features a diamond shape with high balance, creating a power-focused racket that excels in aggressive play. The diamond shape concentrates weight in the upper portion for maximum power generation, while the high balance favors powerful smashes and aggressive volleys. This combination makes the racket ideal for players who want to dominate with power.

Materials and Core

The Metalbone 3.4 utilizes a Carbon Aluminized 2-to-1 frame with carbon fiber faces and an EVA Soft Performance core. The Carbon Aluminized frame provides exceptional stiffness and energy transfer, while the EVA Soft Performance core offers a responsive feel that enhances power. This combination creates a racket that's both powerful and responsive.

Technologies

Adidas's Carbon Aluminized 2-to-1 technology enhances energy transfer and stability, while the EVA Soft Performance core provides excellent responsiveness. The combination of these technologies creates a racket that delivers maximum power with excellent energy transfer, making it ideal for aggressive players.

On the Court

From the Back of the Court

The Metalbone 3.4 offers powerful performance from the back, though it requires good technique to control. The diamond shape and high balance demand precision, but when you connect cleanly, the results are devastating. The racket generates exceptional power on defensive shots, allowing you to turn defense into offense quickly.

Advanced players will appreciate the power available from the back, though the smaller sweet spot requires consistent technique. The racket rewards good timing and positioning with exceptional power, making it ideal for aggressive players.

At the Net

At the net, the Metalbone 3.4 truly excels. The high balance and diamond shape provide exceptional power on volleys and trays, allowing you to finish points decisively. Despite its power focus, the racket maintains enough control for precise net play when you're in position.

Quick exchanges at the net favor this racket, and the power allows you to dominate net play. The responsive carbon fiber construction ensures that every volley carries significant weight, making it difficult for opponents to handle your shots.

On Smash

This is where the Metalbone 3.4 truly shines. The combination of diamond shape, high balance, and Carbon Aluminized frame creates explosive power on smashes. The racket transfers energy efficiently, enabling devastating overheads that can end points immediately.

Advanced players will find the smash power exceptional, allowing them to finish points with authority. The Metalbone 3.4 rewards aggressive play with exceptional results, making it ideal for players who want to dominate with power.

Performance Breakdown

Power (9.0/10)

The Metalbone 3.4 excels in power generation. The diamond shape, high balance, and Carbon Aluminized frame combine to create one of the most powerful rackets available. This is perfect for players who want to dominate with aggressive, powerful shots.

Control (8.5/10)

While power is the Metalbone 3.4's primary strength, control is still very good for a diamond-shaped racket. The EVA Soft Performance core provides enough dampening to maintain feel, and the carbon frame offers excellent feedback. Placement is precise when you're in position, though the smaller sweet spot requires consistent technique.

Rebound (8.5/10)

The EVA Soft Performance core and Carbon Aluminized construction provide excellent energy return. The racket responds well to ball impact, transferring energy efficiently for powerful shots. The rebound quality supports the aggressive playing style this racket is designed for.

Maneuverability (8.0/10)

For a power racket, the Metalbone 3.4 offers good maneuverability. The Carbon Aluminized frame keeps weight manageable, and while the high balance affects quick reactions slightly, advanced players will find it responsive enough for fast-paced play.

Sweet Spot (8.0/10)

The diamond shape results in a smaller sweet spot compared to round or teardrop rackets. This requires more precision and consistent technique. While this makes the racket less forgiving, it rewards clean contact with exceptional power and control.

Who It's For

The Adidas Metalbone 3.4 is designed for advanced players who:

  • Prioritize power and aggressive play
  • Have developed consistent technique
  • Play at the net frequently
  • Want maximum shot speed and penetration
  • Are comfortable with a demanding racket

This racket is not recommended for beginners or intermediate players who are still developing their technique. The small sweet spot and high balance require good timing and positioning to use effectively.
