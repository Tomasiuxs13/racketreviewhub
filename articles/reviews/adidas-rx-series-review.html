Adidas RX Series

2025 Edition

The Adidas RX Series 2025 is designed as a beginner-friendly racket that prioritizes comfort and durability. Featuring a round shape with low balance, a soft EVA core, and an oversized face, this racket provides an excellent foundation for players starting their padel journey. The RX Series proves that you don't need to spend a fortune to get a comfortable, durable racket that helps you learn and improve.

Technical Analysis

Shape and Balance

The RX Series features a round shape with low balance, creating a comfort-focused racket that's easy to handle. The round shape provides a large sweet spot and excellent control, while the low balance enhances maneuverability. The oversized face increases the hitting area, making it more forgiving for beginners.

Materials and Core

The RX Series utilizes a carbon fiber frame with fiberglass faces and a soft EVA core. The fiberglass surface provides a soft feel that's very forgiving, while the soft EVA core offers maximum comfort and reduces vibrations. The lighter weight (355g) makes it easy to handle for extended play sessions.

Technologies

Adidas's entry-level technologies focus on providing comfort and durability. The oversized face increases the hitting area, while the soft EVA core reduces vibrations significantly. The combination of these features creates a racket that's both comfortable and durable for learning.

On the Court

From the Back of the Court

The RX Series offers excellent comfort from the back, thanks to its soft core and oversized face. The round shape and low balance make it easy to control defensive shots, while the comfortable feel makes extended play sessions enjoyable. The oversized face provides more forgiveness, making it easier to make contact with the ball.

Beginners will appreciate the forgiving nature of the racket, making it easier to learn proper technique. The excellent comfort reduces fatigue, allowing for longer practice sessions.

At the Net

At the net, the RX Series provides good control with excellent comfort. The round shape and low balance make it easy to place volleys precisely, while the oversized face makes quick exchanges more manageable. The racket maintains good control for basic net play, making it ideal for beginners developing their net game.

The excellent maneuverability makes it ideal for quick reactions and defensive blocks. The comfortable feel enhances the overall playing experience, making net play more enjoyable for beginners.

On Smash

The smash power is adequate for beginners, thanks to the round shape and balanced design. While not as powerful as premium rackets, the RX Series provides enough power for effective smashes when combined with proper technique. The oversized face makes it easier to make contact, which is particularly helpful for beginners.

Beginners will find the smash power more than adequate for their needs, and the forgiving nature of the racket makes it easier to execute smashes consistently. The balanced design helps beginners learn proper smash technique.

Performance Breakdown

Power (7.0/10)

The RX Series offers adequate power for beginners. The round shape provides a balance between power and control, while the soft core slightly reduces power but enhances comfort. This is a capable racket for entry-level play.

Control (8.0/10)

Control is good for an entry-level racket. The round shape and oversized face make it easy to place shots, while the forgiving feel enhances control. The racket responds well to your intentions, making it ideal for learning.

Rebound (7.5/10)

The soft EVA core and fiberglass construction provide decent energy return. While not as explosive as premium materials, the rebound quality supports both defensive and offensive play effectively for beginners. The comfortable feel enhances the overall playing experience.

Maneuverability (8.5/10)

Maneuverability is excellent, one of the RX Series's strengths. The low balance and lightweight construction make the racket feel fast and responsive, allowing for quick reactions. This makes it ideal for beginners developing their game.

Sweet Spot (8.8/10)

The round shape and oversized face create a very large sweet spot that's extremely forgiving for beginners. The soft core enhances the sweet spot feel, making it easier to find consistently. This makes the racket easier to use and more consistent for learning.

Who It's For

The Adidas RX Series is designed for beginners and intermediate players who:

  • Are just starting their padel journey
  • Want maximum comfort and forgiveness
  • Need a durable racket for learning
  • Want an oversized face for easier contact
  • Have a limited budget

This racket is not recommended for advanced players seeking maximum performance, as the beginner-focused design prioritizes comfort and forgiveness over specialized performance.

Conclusion

If you're looking for comfort and durability at an affordable price, the RX Series is an excellent choice.
