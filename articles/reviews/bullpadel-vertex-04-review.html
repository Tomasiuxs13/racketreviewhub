Bullpadel Vertex 04

2025 Edition

The Bullpadel Vertex 04 2025 represents the evolution of the Vertex series, taking the proven power-focused design and enhancing it with improved aerodynamics and refined technologies. Used by professional players Maxi Sánchez, Gemma Triay, and Arturo Coello, this racket has been battle-tested at the highest levels of competition. The Vertex 04 builds upon the success of the Vertex 03, offering even more power and better maneuverability through enhanced design.

Technical Analysis

Shape and Balance

The Vertex 04 maintains the diamond shape and high balance that define the Vertex series, but with refined aerodynamics that improve swing speed. The diamond shape continues to concentrate weight in the upper portion for maximum power, while the enhanced design reduces air resistance during swings.

Materials and Core

The Vertex 04 features a CarbonTube frame with Xtend Carbon 12K faces and a MultiEVA core. The construction maintains the premium quality of the Vertex series while incorporating refinements that improve performance. The 12K carbon fiber provides exceptional stiffness and durability, while the MultiEVA core offers a balanced response.

Technologies

Bullpadel's latest technologies are on full display in the Vertex 04. Enhanced aerodynamics improve swing speed without sacrificing power, while refined CarbonTube construction provides increased rigidity. The improved design reduces air resistance, making the racket feel faster despite maintaining its power-focused characteristics.

On the Court

From the Back of the Court

The Vertex 04 offers improved playability from the back compared to the Vertex 03, thanks to enhanced aerodynamics. The faster swing speed makes it easier to react to fast shots, while the power remains exceptional. The diamond shape and high balance still require good technique, but the improved design makes it slightly more manageable.

Advanced players will appreciate the enhanced responsiveness, allowing for quicker transitions from defense to offense. The power remains devastating when you connect cleanly, making it ideal for players who want maximum power with improved maneuverability.

At the Net

At the net, the Vertex 04 truly excels. The enhanced aerodynamics make quick reactions easier, while the power remains exceptional. The high balance and diamond shape deliver devastating volleys and trays, and the improved design makes the racket feel faster and more responsive.

The professional endorsements validate the racket's performance at the net, where quick exchanges and powerful finishes are essential. The Vertex 04 allows you to dominate net play with both speed and power.

On Smash

The smash power is exceptional, representing an improvement over the Vertex 03. The enhanced aerodynamics allow for faster racket head speed, while the diamond shape and high balance create explosive power. The combination results in devastating overheads that can end points immediately.

This is where the Vertex 04's improvements are most evident. The faster swing speed combined with exceptional power creates one of the most effective smash rackets available. Advanced players will find the smash performance unmatched.

Performance Breakdown

Power (9.2/10)

The Vertex 04 offers exceptional power, representing an improvement over the Vertex 03. The enhanced design and refined technologies create one of the most powerful rackets available, perfect for players who want to dominate with aggressive play.

Control (8.2/10)

Control is very good for a power racket of this caliber. The MultiEVA core provides enough dampening to maintain feel, and the carbon frame offers excellent feedback. While the small sweet spot requires precision, the improved design makes control slightly better than the Vertex 03.

Rebound (8.5/10)

The MultiEVA core and carbon fiber construction provide excellent energy return. The enhanced design improves energy transfer, resulting in powerful shots with efficient energy use. The rebound quality supports the aggressive playing style perfectly.

Maneuverability (8.0/10)

Maneuverability is improved compared to the Vertex 03, thanks to enhanced aerodynamics. The faster swing speed makes the racket feel more responsive, while the power-focused design remains. Advanced players will appreciate the improved balance between power and speed.

Sweet Spot (7.5/10)

The diamond shape still results in a smaller sweet spot, but the enhanced design makes it slightly more forgiving than the Vertex 03. The improved aerodynamics help with timing, making it easier to find the sweet spot consistently.

Who It's For

The Bullpadel Vertex 04 is designed for advanced players who:

  • Want maximum power with improved maneuverability
  • Play at the professional or high competitive level
  • Dominate at the net with aggressive play
  • Want the latest technology and design improvements
  • Are comfortable with a demanding, power-focused racket

This racket is not recommended for beginners or intermediate players, as it requires excellent technique and timing to use effectively.
